Printer Not Printing

Hardware troubleshooting guide

Knowledge Base / Hardware / Printer Not Printing

Problem

The printer does not print documents even when a print command is sent.

Symptoms

  • Print jobs remain in the queue.
  • Printer makes noise but does not print.
  • Blank pages come out.
  • Printer displays error messages.
  • Documents fail to print.

Possible causes

  • Printer is out of paper.
  • Low or empty ink/toner.
  • Incorrect printer selected.
  • Printer driver problems.
  • Paper jam.
  • Connection issues.
  • Print spooler service failure.

Resolution

  1. Check printer power and connections.
  2. Confirm paper and ink/toner levels.
  3. Clear stuck print jobs:
    • Open printer settings.
    • Cancel pending documents.
  4. Restart the printer and computer.
  5. Update or reinstall printer drivers.
  6. Run printer troubleshooting tools.
  7. Check for paper jams and remove blocked paper.

Prevention

  • Keep printer drivers updated.
  • Clean printer components regularly.
  • Use recommended paper types.
  • Replace ink or toner before it runs out.
  • Turn off the printer properly.
