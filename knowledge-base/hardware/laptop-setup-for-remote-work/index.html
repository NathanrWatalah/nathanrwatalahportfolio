Laptop Setup for Remote Work

Hardware troubleshooting guide

Knowledge Base / Hardware / Laptop Setup for Remote Work

Problem

Practical checks for laptop setup for remote work. Follow the safe steps below and contact your support team if the issue continues.

These are general troubleshooting checks, not a confirmed diagnosis. Follow your organization's procedures and stop if a step could risk data, safety, or managed equipment.

Symptoms to check

  • The laptop does not connect reliably, the display feels uncomfortable, or accessories are not detected.
  • The issue may affect one user device or a group of staff doing remote work.

Possible causes

  • Missing drivers, weak battery or connectivity, or poorly configured accessories may reduce remote productivity.
  • Network, login, or power issues can make a remote setup unreliable.

Resolution

  1. Check the laptop battery, charger, and power port. Confirm the battery is charging and the device is not overheating during normal use.
  2. Verify the docking station, monitor, keyboard, and mouse are properly connected and recognized by the operating system.
  3. Update the operating system and drivers from the official vendor source. If a remote worker uses a VPN or remote access tool, ensure it is installed and tested.
  4. Confirm the laptop is connected to a stable internet connection and that the employee's account has the required permissions and MFA setup.
  5. If the device still fails to support remote work, capture the exact accessory issue, error text, and one screen of diagnostics before opening a support ticket.

Prevention and preparation

  • Use approved laptops, chargers, and docking accessories only.
  • Keep a remote-work checklist for power, internet, and access requirements.
