Power Outage Recovery

Hardware troubleshooting guide

Knowledge Base / Hardware / Power Outage Recovery

Problem

Practical checks for power outage recovery. Follow the safe steps below and contact your support team if the issue continues.

These are general troubleshooting checks, not a confirmed diagnosis. Follow your organization's procedures and stop if a step could risk data, safety, or managed equipment.

Symptoms to check

  • Devices shut down unexpectedly, power returns intermittently, or equipment does not restart cleanly.
  • The issue may affect a single workstation, a circuit, or a whole office area after a power event.

Possible causes

  • A utility outage, surge event, unstable power source, or UPS issue may leave devices in an unsafe state.
  • Damaged power strips, cable wear, or unstable sockets can prevent full recovery.

Resolution

  1. Check whether power is stable at the wall outlet, UPS, or power strip. Confirm no breaker has tripped or any equipment shows a fault light.
  2. Inspect cables and surge protection devices for damage before reconnecting equipment. If any component smells burnt, feels hot, or shows sparks, stop and report it.
  3. Power on devices one at a time starting with the network equipment, then the workstation, and only after the power is stable. Do not reconnect too many devices at once.
  4. Verify important devices are not corrupted by an incomplete shutdown. Run a quick health check and confirm backups were completed before the outage.
  5. If several devices failed together, record the affected area, outage timing, and any UPS or breaker status, then escalate to support or facilities for assessment.

Prevention and preparation

  • Use surge protection, UPS backup where available, and routine electrical safety checks.
  • Keep a documented recovery checklist for after a power outage.
