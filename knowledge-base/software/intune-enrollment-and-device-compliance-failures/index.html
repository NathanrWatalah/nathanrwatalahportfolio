Intune Enrollment and Device Compliance Failures

Software troubleshooting guide

Knowledge Base / Software / Intune Enrollment and Device Compliance Failures

Problem

A device cannot enroll in Intune, does not show as compliant, or is blocked from accessing company resources because it fails required policy checks.

Intune issues often happen when device registration is incomplete, the policy is misconfigured, or the endpoint is missing security settings required by the organization.

Symptoms to check

  • Device shows as not compliant, pending, or not enrolled in the management portal.
  • User cannot access email, Teams, or company apps.
  • Device enrollment fails at setup or after a reset.

Possible causes

  • A required application, password policy, or security setting is not met.
  • Device registration is incomplete or the user account lacks enrollment rights.
  • Corporate Wi-Fi, VPN, or identity settings are blocking the enrollment workflow.

Resolution

  1. Confirm the device is powered on, connected to the internet, and signed in with the correct organizational account.
  2. Review the device status in Intune or the endpoint management portal. Check whether the enrollment is blocked by a policy, device certificate, or compliance rule.
  3. Verify the user’s account is authorized to enroll the device and that the current device is supported by the company’s platform.
  4. Resolve any device compliance issues such as missing encryption, out-of-date OS, or security requirement failures before reattempting enrollment.
  5. If the portal still reports an error, collect the enrollment error code and device details, then escalate to the identity or endpoint management team.

Prevention and preparation

  • Keep device enrollment policies aligned with the hardware and OS approved by the organization.
  • Regularly verify the endpoint management team is tracking noncompliant devices before they block access.
  • Document the supported enrollment steps and common compliance failures for users.
