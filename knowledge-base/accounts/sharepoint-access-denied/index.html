SharePoint Access Denied

Accounts troubleshooting guide

Knowledge Base / Accounts / SharePoint Access Denied

Problem

Practical checks for SharePoint access denied. Follow the safe steps below and contact your support team if the issue continues.

These are general troubleshooting checks, not a confirmed diagnosis. Follow your organization's procedures and stop if a step could risk data, safety, or managed equipment.

Symptoms to check

  • A user cannot open a site, document, or library even though they recently had access.
  • The issue may affect one site or multiple users depending on the permission change.

Possible causes

  • Permission changes, group membership updates, or a recently changed site owner may block access.
  • Incorrect user type, license status, or a stale sync with the identity system may also cause denial.

Resolution

  1. Confirm the user is signed in with the correct account and that their Microsoft 365 or SharePoint license is active.
  2. Check whether the access issue is site-specific or affects several locations. If the user can access other sites, the problem may be limited to one library, folder, or permission group.
  3. Verify the user is in the correct group or permission role and that the site owner or admin has not recently changed permissions.
  4. If the issue is in a shared library, ask the site owner to confirm the user’s access level and review whether the item is checked out, restricted, or inherited from a parent folder.
  5. Escalate to the SharePoint admin with the affected site, exact error, and the user’s role and permission details so the team can review the policy configuration.

Prevention and preparation

  • Keep permission changes documented and review site access when users move roles or leave the team.
  • Use groups instead of assigning permissions one by one for easier maintenance.
